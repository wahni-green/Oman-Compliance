# Oman VAT Compliance — Findings & Requirements

Audit date: 2026-08-23
Source reviewed: `oman_vat` app at `/home/frappe/frappe-bench/apps/oman_vat` (upstream `wahni-green/Oman-Compliance`, latest commit April 2022)

This document captures the findings from a compliance/code audit of the legacy `oman_vat` app, and the regulatory requirements it should be checked against, to inform the design of this app (`oman_compliance`).

## Verdict on the legacy app

By its own README, `oman_vat` is "a minor tweak" of a Saudi Arabia VAT app (`ksa_vat`), ported to Oman and not updated since April 2022. It provides a sales/purchase VAT ledger report and a decorative QR code on invoices, but does not implement Oman's actual VAT return structure, reverse charge, designated-zone rules, or currency conversion to OMR — and has no path toward the mandatory e-invoicing regime (Fawtara/PINT-OM) the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) is rolling out from 2026–2027.

It works narrowly as an internal sales/purchase-by-tax-template ledger for a single-currency OMR company that manually maintains its own VAT settings. Outside that case — multi-currency invoices, reverse-charge purchases, designated-zone trade, anything touching e-invoicing — it either produces wrong numbers or does nothing.

## 1. Regulatory baseline (Oman VAT law)

Oman's VAT regime runs on the VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020) and its Executive Regulations, administered by the OTA.

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Standard rate | 5%, applied since 16 April 2021. |
| Registration | Mandatory above OMR 38,500 in annual taxable supplies (rolling 12 months); voluntary from OMR 19,250. Non-residents register from their first taxable supply, no threshold. |
| Filing | Quarterly VAT return and payment, due 30 days after quarter-end, filed through the OTA e-services portal. Larger/specific taxpayers may be moved to monthly. |
| Return structure | Seven sections: (1) domestic supplies, (2) purchases under reverse charge, (3) supplies outside Oman, (4) imports of goods, (5) total VAT due, (6) input VAT credit, (7) net tax liability. |
| Tax invoice content | "Tax Invoice" must appear on the document; supplier name, address and VATIN are mandatory; bilingual (Arabic/English) content is expected in practice. A simplified tax invoice ("Simplified Tax Invoice" wording) is permitted under OMR 500 excl. VAT for supplies to non-taxable consumers. |
| Zero-rated / exempt / out-of-scope | Three distinct categories — basic food, healthcare, education and similar zero-rated items; exempt supplies (e.g. certain financial services, bare land); and out-of-scope transactions. Each has different return treatment. |
| Reverse charge | VAT-registered recipients self-account for VAT on imported services and other specified reverse-charge supplies — reported in its own return box, not as ordinary output tax. |
| Designated/special zones | Duqm (SEZAD), and the free zones at Salalah, Sohar and Al Mazunah are treated as outside Oman for VAT. Supply into, out of, or within a zone can be zero-rated under Article 54, subject to conditions on the recipient. |
| Currency | Accounting records and the VAT return must be in OMR. Foreign-currency invoices must state the exchange rate used (Central Bank of Oman average rate on the tax due date) to convert to OMR. |
| Record retention | 10 years generally; 15 years for real-estate-related records. |

### E-invoicing mandate (Fawtara / PINT-OM) — time-sensitive

Under Decision No. 189/2026, the OTA is rolling out mandatory structured e-invoicing through its **Fawtara** platform, using **PINT-OM** (Oman's adaptation of the Peppol international standard):

- Voluntary pilot with ~100 companies begins **late August 2026**.
- Taxpayers with annual supplies over OMR 5 million must comply from **1 April 2027**.
- All other VAT-registered businesses must comply from **1 October 2027**.
- Invoices must be issued as structured XML (UBL 2.1 / PINT-OM) plus a human-readable PDF/A-3, sent to an OTA-accredited Service Provider within 24 hours, typically over the Peppol network.
- A QR code is mandatory on the human-readable invoice for B2C transactions — generated by the taxpayer, not embedded in the XML.

| OMR currency conversion | **Missing — active bug** | Report columns are labelled "Amount (OMR)" but sum `item.net_amount` (document currency) instead of `base_net_amount`. Any foreign-currency invoice reports the wrong figure. No exchange-rate disclosure logic. |
| Sequential invoice numbering | Adequate | Delegated to core ERPNext naming series. |

| Auto-create VAT settings on new company | **Missing — dead code** | Duplicate dict key in `hooks.py` silently disables the intended behaviour (see bugs below). |
| Record retention / archival | Not applicable to software | A business duty; standard Frappe document history is a reasonable base. |
| Automated tests for VAT calculations | **Missing** | Test files are empty stub classes with `pass`. No coverage of actual calculation logic. |

## 3. Bugs found in the legacy app (for reference — avoid repeating)

- **Duplicate dict key silently disables auto-setup** (`hooks.py:92-100`) — `doc_events["Company"]` defines `"on_update"` twice; Python keeps only the second, so `create_oman_vat_setting` never actually runs.
- **VAT report ignores currency conversion** (`report/oman_vat/oman_vat.py:137-143`) — sums `net_amount` instead of `base_net_amount` despite OMR-labelled columns.
- **Undeclared runtime dependency** (`events/accounts/sales_invoice.py:5`) — imports `pyqrcode` directly but it's absent from `requirements.txt`.
- **Dead functions / orphaned fields** — `delete_vat_settings_for_company()` and `update_regional_tax_settings()` defined but never called (the latter would raise `TypeError` if it were); `oman_einv_qr`, `is_zero_rated`, `is_exempt` fields created but never read anywhere.
- **Fixture/hooks mismatch** (`hooks.py:186,188`) — references fields (`Customer-arabic_name`, `Contact-is_billing_contact`) that don't exist in the actual code.
- **Stale KSA print formats left in place, disabled** — still carry Saudi-specific `tax_code` field and mislabelled "Reverse Charge Applicable".
- **No migration patches** despite custom fields added across ~10 doctypes over the app's life.
- **Packaging gaps**: no `pyproject.toml`; `requirements.txt` is a single comment line; `license.txt` is one line (`License: MIT`) with no actual license body; `hooks.py` has no `required_apps`, no `minimum_frappe_version`/`minimum_erpnext_version`.

## 4. Requirements for this app (`oman_compliance`)

Derived directly from the gaps above — what a compliant replacement needs to do:

1. **VAT return generation** matching the OTA's 7-section structure (domestic supplies, reverse-charge purchases, supplies outside Oman, imports of goods, total VAT due, input VAT credit, net tax liability) — not just a sales/purchase ledger.
2. **Full supply categorization**: standard-rated, zero-rated, exempt, **and out-of-scope** (the last was entirely absent in the legacy app).
3. **Reverse charge mechanism**: a real flag/field on purchase transactions, tax logic that self-accounts output VAT on the recipient side, and its own return box.
4. **Designated/special zone handling**: Duqm (SEZAD), Salalah, Sohar, Al Mazunah free zones — treat as outside Oman per Article 54 conditions.
5. **Correct OMR reporting**: always use company-currency (`base_*`) amounts for VAT figures; disclose the Central Bank of Oman exchange rate used for any foreign-currency invoice.
6. **Tax invoice compliance**: bilingual (EN/AR) content, "Tax Invoice" wording, supplier name/address/VATIN, and automatic switch to "Simplified Tax Invoice" wording under the OMR 500 threshold for B2C supplies.
7. **TRN/VATIN validation**: format/length validation on the company and customer/supplier tax ID fields, not just a passthrough of `tax_id`.
8. **E-invoicing (Fawtara/PINT-OM)** — the most time-sensitive item:
   - Structured XML invoice generation (UBL 2.1 / PINT-OM).
   - Human-readable PDF/A-3 output.
   - Integration path to an OTA-accredited Service Provider (Peppol network), invoice submission within 24 hours of issuance.
   - QR code on the human-readable invoice for B2C transactions.
   - Target readiness well before the 1 April 2027 (large taxpayers) / 1 October 2027 (all others) deadlines.
9. **Packaging hygiene**: proper `pyproject.toml` (already present in this app), pinned dependencies, `required_apps`/version bounds in `hooks.py`, real license text.
10. **Test coverage** for VAT calculation logic, currency conversion, and any auto-setup hooks — the legacy app's bugs went unnoticed for years partly because none of this was tested.
